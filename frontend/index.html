Questions

How is my plan built?

Your goal, training days, equipment and diet preference are used to pick a workout split and a daily meal plan on the server.

Do I need a gym?

No. Choose Full Gym, Home Gym, Dumbbells Only or Bodyweight Only, and your workouts match what you have.

Is this medical advice?

No. FitForge is for general fitness education. Talk to a doctor about injuries, conditions or big diet changes.
